# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment Script: Miami Art Week Exhibition Opportunities

**Style:** Casual but authoritative friend over coffee at the café. Encouraging, uplifting, never punching down.

---

Hey, so grab your coffee — or whatever’s in that cup — and let me catch you up on the Miami Art Week exhibition scene for artists this year. It’s November 26th, 2026, so the big week is right around the corner: Miami Art Week runs roughly November 30 through December 6, with Art Basel Miami Beach public days December 4-6 at the Convention Center.

I’ve been digging through the web, X, Instagram, and open-call sites looking specifically for free opportunities — no cost to the artist, clear deadlines, and ones that actually get you into the event or exhibition. We prefer zero-fee, but if something’s truly prestigious or gives you something solid in return, we’ll note the fee and why it might still be worth it.

Here’s the honest coffee-table truth: by late November, most of the truly free open calls for exhibiting during the week have already closed. Deadlines tend to hit in September and October so organizers can curate and install. But that doesn’t mean the scene is closed — it means the smart play is knowing what just happened, what’s still possibly flexible, and where to look for next year’s free ones. Let’s walk through the standouts.

**First up, the pure free digital one that closed but shows what’s possible:** Render Foundation’s Miami Art Week 2026 Open Call. Deadline was October 30 — closed about a month ago. No application fee at all. They were selecting 10-15 artists for a 50-foot large-scale projection exhibition on Miami Beach on December 2. Digital and visual arts welcome, any nationality. Selected works get rendered on the Render Network with credits covering the costs. That’s real exhibition exposure to the Art Basel crowds plus technical support. Google “Render Foundation Miami Art Week open call” or check opencalls.ai — the Typeform is closed now, but the model is gold for future digital/Web3-adjacent projection work.

**Web3/DAO flavor:** One Love Art DAO’s FRONTERA open call. They posted on X around late October, deadline November 15. Google Form link was shared. Looking for works that challenge understanding and shift perspectives. Selected artists exhibited at their gallery at the YVE Hotel during the week, plus possible partner opportunities. Seems free — social submission style, no fee mentioned. They announced selected artists on November 25. Follow @OneLoveArtDAO on X and Instagram for next year; they do contemporary and digital-leaning shows. Details at onelovedao.org and their Luma events.

**Print/visibility free-ish:** Art Miami Magazine’s open call for their Special Print Edition distributed during Art Week. Deadline October 20. Requires a full Artist Bio *and* Artist Statement (applications without both are discarded), plus Instagram and/or website. Open to any nationality, 18+, any career stage. No fee listed. Form is a Google Form. Search “Art Miami Magazine open call visual artists 2026” — the page is still up if you want to see the requirements. Great for portfolio building even if the deadline passed.

**Public art free:** SaveArtSpace “As Above, So Below” for bus shelter ads in Miami during the week. Deadline October 19. Theme around cosmic/earthly patterns. Selected artists announced; public installations opening around November 27. Donation-based in some of their calls, but this one was framed as free to apply. Check saveartspace.org for ongoing public ad-space calls year-round.

**Local low-fee example (for completeness):** Backyard Basel at Coral Gables Museum, December 4 Gallery Night. Local artists 18+, diverse media, works priced $50-$5,000. $50 application fee + $50 exhibition fee if selected. Upload three images. Still listed as of late November — check coralgablesmuseum.org. Not free, but local, sales-focused, and entry to the event.

**Prestigious with fee (included because of what you get):** Downtown Miami Art Week (Aura Copeland galleries / Les Couleurs) — multi-location exhibition December 1-6 on East Flagler. Artist registration via Moonclerk (payment platform), so wall-space fee involved. Delivery window was Nov 5-25. Rolling-ish in earlier posts. Three gallery spaces, VIP preview Nov 30. Prestigious downtown presence with Mana Common support. Google “Downtown Miami Art Week 2026 artist registration lescouleursmiamibeach.com”. Fee is for actual wall space and curated placement during the peak week — that’s the clear reason.

Fridge Art Fair (Gianni • Madonna • Coconuts theme) had a $35 entry fee open call for small 2D works, deadline extended to Nov 15. Multiple South Beach venues Dec 1-6. Fun, experimental, free public entry in many hours. Check fridgeartfair.com.

**Where to look going forward / Google these:**
- opencalls.ai + “Miami Art Week”
- X search: “Miami Art Week open call” or from:OneLoveArtDAO
- Instagram: #MiamiArtWeekOpenCall or accounts like @fridge_art_fair_official, @aura_copeland
- miamiartweek.org for their arteNXT digital (has fees)
- City of Miami Beach No Vacancy (juried, local, free public install)

The big takeaway: free exhibition slots during the week are competitive and close early. The ones that stay free usually lean digital, public, DAO/community, or print/visibility. Physical gallery space almost always involves a fee once accepted. Keep your portfolio ready, bio + statement polished, and follow the Web3 galleries and public-art orgs — next year’s free calls will drop in summer/fall.

You’ve got this. The energy in Miami that week is real, and there are still ways to be part of it even if the pure free open calls have wrapped. Check the database for the full list with exact links and status. More opportunities drop daily — we’ll keep updating.

Cheers — go make something that shifts a perspective.
